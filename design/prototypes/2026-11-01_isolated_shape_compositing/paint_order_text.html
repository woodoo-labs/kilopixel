1 · Current: interleaved per line

fill L1 → stroke L1 → fill L2 → stroke L2 …
Lower lines cover the outline of upper lines.

2 · Two passes: all fill, then all stroke

Same as applyStyle / SVG default / CSS text-stroke.
Outlines are always on top.

3 · paintorder="stroke": stroke under fill

All strokes first, fills on top.
Glyphs keep their full shape; only the outer half of the outline shows.

A) Multi-line, overlapping lines (lineheight 0.72), strokewidth 7: watch where descenders (y, p, j) meet the next line
B) Single line on a busy background, strokewidth 10 (options 1 and 2 are identical here)
