Negative sweep: what the arrowheads do

Real <pxl-circle> and <pxl-ellipse> arcs with the engine's arrowheads (shaft trimmed under the heads), arrowheads 44 at both ends, stroke 14. The blue dot is the start. B: today, sweep="S". A1: the sign is the direction, written today as sweep="S" anticlockwise="S < 0". A2: like A1, and the heads shrink while the arc is shorter than both heads (the arc length, measured along the curve).

Ellipse, A2 two ways: heads shrunk by the arc length (left) or by r × |sweep| with r = (rx + ry) / 2 = 85 (right)
