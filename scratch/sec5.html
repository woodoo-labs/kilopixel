5. Radial Gradient (6-Value Mode)

The 6-value mode unlocks the classic "3D Spotlight" effect. In HTML5 canvas, radial gradients are actually defined by two circles: an inner circle and an outer circle. In this mode, the inner circle collapses to a single Focal Point (cx0, cy0), while the outer circle is defined by its own center (cx1, cy1) and radius point (rx1, ry1).

By moving the focal point away from the outer circle's center, the gradient converges towards that specific spot, creating the illusion of 3D spheres, lighting spotlights, or directional glows.

6-Value Mode Syntax: (Normalized coordinates)

fill="radial([cx0, cy0, cx1, cy1, rx1, ry1], colorsArray)"

In this example, the green coordinate represents the Focal Point (where the gradient originates). Try moving it independently from the outer circle's center!

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <pxl-rect 
      x="500" y="300"
      w="400" h="250" 
      rotate="0"
      fill="radial([0.2, 0.2, 0.5, 0.5, 1, 1], ['#e2e8f0', '#475569'])">
    </pxl-rect>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
