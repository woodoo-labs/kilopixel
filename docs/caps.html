Caps & Joins

Control how strokes terminate at open endpoints and how connected segments meet at vertices. Kilopixel provides declarative control over linecap, linejoin, and miterlimit across both open lines and closed geometry.

Open Paths vs. Vertices

linecap governs the termination of open endpoints (such as <pxl-line>, open <pxl-polyline>, or arc segments). linejoin and miterlimit govern the outer corners where two connected segments meet at a vertex.

1. Line Caps

The linecap attribute controls the ending shape of open paths — including <pxl-line>, open <pxl-polyline>, and open circular arcs (<pxl-circle> with sweep or start/end). Kilopixel supports three standard cap styles:

  • Butt: The default butt style cuts the stroke flush exactly at the path coordinates. The stroke ends strictly at the endpoints without extending past them.
  • Round: The round style adds a semicircular cap extending past each endpoint by half the stroke width (radius = strokewidth / 2).
  • Square: The square style adds a rectangular cap extending past each endpoint by half the stroke width.

Open Paths vs. Closed Shapes

linecap only affects paths with open endpoints. Closed shapes (such as <pxl-rect> or full 360° circles) close their subpaths automatically, so line caps are not rendered on them.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- 1. Straight Line -->
    <pxl-line linecap="butt" strokewidth="40" rotate="0" x="300" y="300" x1="-150" y1="0" x2="150" y2="0" stroke="#f97316"></pxl-line>

    <!-- 2. Circular Arc -->
    <pxl-circle linecap="butt" strokewidth="40" sweep="270" r="150" start="135" x="700" y="300" stroke="#3b82f6" fill="none"></pxl-circle>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

2. Line Joins

The linejoin attribute determines how two connected stroke segments meet at a corner or vertex. Kilopixel supports three standard corner join styles:

  • Miter: The default miter style extends the outer edges of the stroke segments until they intersect in a sharp point.
  • Round: The round style rounds off the corner with a circular curve having a radius equal to half the stroke width.
  • Bevel: The bevel style slices off the outer corner with a flat diagonal edge between the outer endpoints of the two segments.

Open vs. Closed Corner Joins

Line joins apply to every vertex where two segments meet — including intermediate vertices of open <pxl-polyline> shapes, as well as the closing joint of closed shapes like <pxl-polyline closed="true"> and <pxl-rect>.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- 1. Open Chevron Corner -->
    <pxl-polyline linejoin="miter" strokewidth="70" points="150,450; 300,150; 450,450" stroke="#f97316" fill="none"></pxl-polyline>

    <!-- 2. Closed Polygon Corner -->
    <pxl-polyline linejoin="round" strokewidth="40" points="550,450; 700,150; 850,450" closed="true" stroke="#3b82f6" fill="#dbeafe66"></pxl-polyline>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

3. Miter Limit

The miterlimit attribute establishes the maximum ratio between the outer point of a corner (miter length) and the stroke width. When two connected line segments join at a sharp acute angle, a miter join extends outward into a needle. To prevent runaway spikes from distorting the design, the engine clips the vertex into a flat bevel whenever the miter ratio exceeds this threshold.

  • Threshold Trigger: When the miter ratio exceeds miterlimit, the sharp point automatically slices into a flat bevel.
  • Default Limit (10): Allows sharp miters on almost all natural angles down to roughly 11.5° while preventing runaway spikes.

The Miter Ratio & Scale Invariance

The geometric miter length is strokewidth / sin(θ / 2). Because the ratio is computed by dividing this length by strokewidth, stroke width cancels out completely: ratio = 1 / sin(θ / 2). This makes clipping purely angle-dependent, ensuring sharp corners behave consistently regardless of stroke thickness or scale.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- 1. Open Chevron Corner -->
    <pxl-polyline miterlimit="10" linejoin="miter" strokewidth="40" points="220,450; 300,150; 380,450" stroke="#f97316" fill="none"></pxl-polyline>

    <!-- 2. Closed Diamond Corner -->
    <pxl-polyline miterlimit="10" linejoin="miter" strokewidth="40" points="700,150; 762,300; 700,450; 638,300" closed="true" stroke="#3b82f6" fill="#dbeafe66"></pxl-polyline>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
