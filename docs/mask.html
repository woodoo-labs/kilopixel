Mask

Use the mask attribute to perform boolean clipping operations (intersections, cutouts, and knockouts) between overlapping shapes.

Shapes Only!

The mask attribute applies strictly to shapes (like <pxl-circle> or <pxl-rect>). Applying a mask to a <pxl-layer> will be ignored.

1. Boolean Operations

In Kilopixel, boolean clipping involves destination shapes (what is already drawn on the layer) and a source shape (what is about to be drawn). By changing the mask mode on the source shape, you dictate how it intersects with the underlying shapes.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- The Destination Shapes -->
    <pxl-rect x="350" y="300" w="400" h="300" rotate="0" alpha="1" fill="darkorange"></pxl-rect>

    <!-- The Source Shape -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="650" y="300" rx="200" ry="150" rotate="0" alpha="1" fill="dodgerblue" mask="xor"></pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
