Ellipse

The ellipse shape renders ovals, concentric rings, and orbital paths. It supports independent horizontal and vertical radii, inner rings, and tangent-aligned arrowheads.

1. Ellipses

An ellipse is defined by its horizontal radius (rx) and vertical radius (ry). Specifying inner horizontal (irx) and vertical (iry) radii hollows out the interior to form a concentric elliptical ring.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Solid Disc -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="300" y="300" rx="180" ry="110" fill="#f97316"></pxl-ellipse>

    <!-- Right: Hollow Ring -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="700" y="300" rx="180" ry="110" irx="90" iry="55" stroke="#3b82f6"></pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

2. Sectors

Setting pie="true" connects the ends of an arc to the center point to create an elliptical pie slice. Combining inner radii (irx, iry) with a sweep or end angle automatically seals outer and inner arcs into a hollow donut wedge.

Sweep vs. End Angle

Kilopixel supports both relative spans and absolute destination angles: sweep defines a relative angular span from start (e.g. start="45" sweep="90" spans 90° to reach 135°), whereas end defines an absolute target angle. If both are specified on the same element, sweep takes priority and end is ignored.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Pie Slice (Sweep Mode) -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="300" y="300" strokewidth="10" pie="true" fill="#ffedd566" stroke="#f97316"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      start="0" sweep="270"></pxl-ellipse>

    <!-- Right: Donut Wedge (End Angle Mode) -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="700" y="300" pie="true" fill="#dbeafe66" stroke="#3b82f6" strokewidth="4"
      rx="180" ry="110" irx="90" iry="55"
      start="0" end="270"></pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

3. Chords

By default, sweeping a partial ellipse creates an Open Chord: the interior is filled, but the stroke only follows the curved perimeter. Setting closed="true" explicitly connects the endpoints with a straight chord, forming a Closed Chord (an elliptical segment or lens cap) where the stroke fully wraps around the straight edge.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Open Chord (Default) -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="300" y="300" strokewidth="10" fill="#ffedd566" stroke="#f97316"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      sweep="270"></pxl-ellipse>

    <!-- Right: Closed Chord (closed="true") -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="700" y="300" strokewidth="10" closed="true" fill="#dbeafe66" stroke="#3b82f6"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      sweep="180"></pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

4. Arcs

By simply omitting the fill attribute, you can use ellipses to draw un-filled, stroked curved lines — perfect for orbits and perspective trajectory paths. The base Shape class also supports linedash and dashoffset, allowing you to easily create dashed orbital tracks and marching ant effects.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Simple Arc (Sweep Mode) -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="300" y="300" stroke="#f97316" strokewidth="10"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      start="0" sweep="180"></pxl-ellipse>

    <!-- Right: Dashed Arc (End Angle Mode) -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="700" y="300" stroke="#3b82f6" strokewidth="4" linedash="[15, 15]" dashoffset="0"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      start="0" end="270"></pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

Controls

5. Arrowheads

Adding arrowheads to an open arc transforms it into a curved vector diagram arrow, perspective flow indicator, or orbital compass. You can independently apply and scale arrows on both the start and end of the path.

Parametric Tangent Alignment

Unlike circles where the tangent is always perpendicular to the radius, an ellipse changes curvature dynamically. Kilopixel computes the instantaneous derivative of the ellipse at every angle (−rx · sin θ, ry · cos θ), guaranteeing that arrowheads remain perfectly tangent to the curve without distortion.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Filled Arrows -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="300" y="300" stroke="#f97316" strokewidth="8"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      start="-45" sweep="270"
      arrowstyle="filled" arrowstart="25" arrowend="25"></pxl-ellipse>

    <!-- Right: Line Arrows -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="700" y="300" stroke="#3b82f6" strokewidth="8" linedash="[15, 15]"
      rx="180" ry="110"
      start="-45" sweep="270"
      arrowstyle="line" arrowstart="0" arrowend="35"></pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
