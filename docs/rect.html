Rectangle

The primary orthogonal primitive in Kilopixel, <pxl-rect> renders sharp boxes, rounded cards, stadium pill buttons, and responsive layouts — with built-in support for 9-position anchor pinning and independent per-corner radii.

1. Dimensions

A rectangle's size is determined by its width (w) and height (h). By default, the shape is centered directly over its x and y coordinates.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Square -->
    <pxl-rect x="300" y="300" w="240" h="240" fill="#ffedd566" stroke="#f97316"></pxl-rect>

    <!-- Right: Rectangle -->
    <pxl-rect x="700" y="300" w="360" h="200" fill="#dbeafe66" stroke="#3b82f6"></pxl-rect>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
