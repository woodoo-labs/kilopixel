Arrowheads

Transform open paths into directional vectors, flow indicators, and dimension lines. Kilopixel supports solid filled triangles and open line chevrons across straight lines, circular arcs, and parametric elliptical curves with automatic proportional scaling or custom pixel sizing.

1. Sizing Arrowheads

Kilopixel provides two modes for sizing arrowheads: automatic scaling ('auto'), where the arrowhead length scales proportionally with stroke thickness (strokewidth × 3.6), and custom numeric sizing, where the arrowhead length is defined explicitly and remains independent of the stroke width.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Auto-Sized Line -->
    <pxl-line x1="150" y1="300" x2="450" y2="300" stroke="#f97316"
      arrowstart="auto" arrowend="auto"
      strokewidth="10">
    </pxl-line>

    <!-- Right: Custom-Sized Sine Wave -->
    <pxl-polyline points="550,300; 650,220; 750,380; 850,300" smooth="1" stroke="#3b82f6"
      arrowstart="0" arrowend="35"
      strokewidth="6">
    </pxl-polyline>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
