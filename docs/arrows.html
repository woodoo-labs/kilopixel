Arrowheads

Transform open paths into directional vectors, flow indicators, and dimension lines. Kilopixel supports solid filled triangles and open line chevrons across straight lines, circular arcs, and parametric elliptical curves with automatic proportional scaling or custom pixel sizing.

1. Endpoints & Placement

Arrowheads are enabled on any open path shape by defining arrowstart and arrowend. You can place an arrowhead on either end individually to indicate a forward or backward vector, or enable both simultaneously to create bidirectional indicators.

Open Paths Only

Arrowheads attach specifically to the endpoints of open stroked paths (<pxl-line>, open <pxl-circle> arcs, open <pxl-ellipse> arcs, and <pxl-polyline>). Closed shapes (such as full 360° circles, rectangles, or polygons) have continuous perimeters without endpoints, so arrowhead attributes are ignored.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: One-Way Arrow -->
    <pxl-line x1="150" y1="300" x2="450" y2="300" stroke="#f97316"
      arrowstart="0" arrowend="auto"
      strokewidth="8" arrowstyle="filled">
    </pxl-line>

    <!-- Right: Bidirectional Arrow -->
    <pxl-line x1="550" y1="300" x2="850" y2="300" stroke="#3b82f6"
      arrowstart="auto" arrowend="auto"
      strokewidth="8" arrowstyle="filled">
    </pxl-line>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
