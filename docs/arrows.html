Arrowheads

Arrowheads turn open paths into arrows. They can be added to either end of lines, polylines, circular arcs and elliptical arcs, drawn as filled triangles (arrowstyle="filled") or open chevrons (arrowstyle="line"), and sized automatically from the stroke width or with a fixed value.

1. Sizing

Arrowheads are added to open paths using the arrowstart and arrowend attributes. Both attributes support two sizing modes: automatic scaling ('auto'), which sets the arrowhead size to strokewidth × 3.6, and custom numeric sizing, where the arrowhead size is defined explicitly and remains independent of the stroke width.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Auto-Sized Circular Arc -->
    <pxl-circle x="300" y="300" r="120" start="135" sweep="270"
      stroke="#f97316" fill="none"
      arrowstart="auto" arrowend="auto"
      strokewidth="10">
    </pxl-circle>

    <!-- Right: Custom-Sized Sine Wave -->
    <pxl-polyline points="550,300; 650,220; 750,380; 850,300" smooth="1" stroke="#3b82f6"
      arrowstart="0" arrowend="35"
      strokewidth="6">
    </pxl-polyline>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

2. Styles

Kilopixel provides two visual styles for arrowheads via the arrowstyle attribute: filled triangles (arrowstyle="filled") and open chevrons (arrowstyle="line"). Both styles use the shape's stroke color, and arrowstyle="line" arrowheads use the shape's stroke width (strokewidth).

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Solid Triangle (Filled) -->
    <pxl-line x1="150" y1="300" x2="450" y2="300" stroke="#f97316"
      arrowend="40" strokewidth="10"
      arrowstyle="filled">
    </pxl-line>

    <!-- Right: Open Chevron (Line) -->
    <pxl-line x1="550" y1="300" x2="850" y2="300" stroke="#3b82f6"
      arrowend="40" strokewidth="10"
      arrowstyle="line">
    </pxl-line>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

3. Line Caps

In canvas graphics, line caps define where a stroked path physically ends. A butt cap cuts flush at the path coordinate, while square and round caps extend past the coordinate by half the stroke width (strokewidth / 2). Kilopixel's arrowhead geometry automatically aligns to this physical boundary:

  • Butt: The stroke terminates flush at the coordinate, and the arrowhead tip lands precisely on the endpoint plane.
  • Square & Round: Both cap styles extend past the endpoint — the exact same behavior as on any open path shape. Arrowheads automatically advance with this cap extension to stay locked to the outer stroke boundary.

This applies to both arrow styles and to every shape that supports arrowheads, including polylines and arcs.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Top: Line -->
    <pxl-line x1="350" y1="150" x2="750" y2="150" stroke="#f97316"
      arrowend="75" strokewidth="20"
      arrowstyle="filled" linecap="butt">
    </pxl-line>

    <!-- Middle: Polyline -->
    <pxl-polyline points="350,300; 450,250; 550,350; 650,300; 750,300" stroke="#3b82f6"
      arrowend="75" strokewidth="20"
      arrowstyle="filled" linecap="square">
    </pxl-polyline>

    <!-- Bottom: Elliptical Arc -->
    <pxl-ellipse x="750" y="580" rx="400" ry="130" start="180" sweep="90" stroke="#22c55e"
      arrowend="75" strokewidth="20"
      arrowstyle="line" linecap="round">
    </pxl-ellipse>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>

4. Line Joins

arrowstyle="line" arrowheads are stroked, so their tip follows the shape's line join (linejoin): miter draws a sharp tip, bevel a flat tip, and round a rounded tip. The tip still ends at the cap boundary described above. With miter, the tip stays sharp even when miterlimit is lowered to blunt the corners of the path. Filled arrowheads are not affected by linejoin.

Sharp Tips on Rounded Paths

Filled arrowheads ignore linejoin. To combine rounded corners with a sharp tip, use arrowstyle="filled".

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Top: Miter Join -->
    <pxl-polyline points="350,185; 390,115; 430,185; 470,115; 510,185; 750,150" stroke="#f97316"
      arrowend="75" strokewidth="20"
      miterlimit="10"
      arrowstyle="line" linejoin="miter">
    </pxl-polyline>

    <!-- Middle: Bevel Join -->
    <pxl-polyline points="350,335; 390,265; 430,335; 470,265; 510,335; 750,300" stroke="#3b82f6"
      arrowend="75" strokewidth="20"
      arrowstyle="line" linejoin="bevel">
    </pxl-polyline>

    <!-- Bottom: Round Join -->
    <pxl-polyline points="350,485; 390,415; 430,485; 470,415; 510,485; 750,450" stroke="#22c55e"
      arrowend="75" strokewidth="20"
      arrowstyle="line" linejoin="round">
    </pxl-polyline>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
