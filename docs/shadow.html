Shadow

Kilopixel features a powerful dual-architecture for shadows: Native Canvas shadows for dynamic shape-level interaction, and CSS drop-shadow filters for unified layer silhouettes.

Shape vs Layer Shadows

The shadowcolor, shadowblur, shadowx, and shadowy attributes apply strictly to shapes. They cause shapes to cast shadows directly onto each other. To cast a unified silhouette shadow of an entire layer onto the page background, use the filter="dropShadow(...)" attribute on the <pxl-layer> instead.

1. Shape Shadows (Native)

Applying the native shadowcolor attribute to a shape utilizes the raw HTML5 Canvas API. This means that as shapes overlap, the top shape will cast a physical shadow onto the shape below it in 2D space.

Notice how the blue circle casts a dark shadow directly across the orange rectangle!

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Bottom Shape -->
    <pxl-rect x="350" y="300" w="400" h="300" fill="#ffedd566" stroke="#f97316" strokewidth="4"></pxl-rect>

    <!-- Top Shape casting a shadow -->
    <pxl-circle x="650" y="300" r="150" fill="#dbeafe66" stroke="#3b82f6" strokewidth="4"
      shadowcolor="hsla(220, 100, 15, 0.7)" shadowblur="20" shadowx="-30" shadowy="20">
    </pxl-circle>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
