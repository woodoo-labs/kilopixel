Polyline

The polyline shape draws connected paths through a list of points. It supports smooth curves, closed and filled outlines, coordinates that animate one by one, and start and end arrowheads.

1. Points

A polyline connects a list of points with straight segments. Points are separated by semicolons and the x and y values of a point by a comma, so points="50,300; 150,150; 250,300; 350,450; 450,300" draws a zig-zag through (50, 300), (150, 150), (250, 300), (350, 450) and (450, 300).

Every x and every y value is its own expression. A value can use math like min(a, b), follow a variable like ref.* or animate on its own like sin(t), while all other points stay where they are: points="50,300; 150,150 + sin(t) * 100; 250,300; 350,450; 450,300" moves only the peak up and down.

<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <pxl-layer>
    <!-- Left: Zig-Zag -->
    <pxl-polyline stroke="#f97316"
      points="50,300; 150,150; 250,300; 350,450; 450,300">
    </pxl-polyline>

    <!-- Right: Spiral -->
    <pxl-polyline stroke="#3b82f6"
      points="550,100; 900,100; 900,450; 650,450; 650,200; 800,200; 800,350">
    </pxl-polyline>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
