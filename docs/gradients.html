Gradients

Kilopixel offers a highly flexible generative gradient system. Learn how to map linear, radial, and conic gradients accurately to your shapes, seamlessly animated and updated at 60fps.

The 0-to-1 Bounding Box: In Kilopixel, all gradients are mapped to an element's invisible bounding box. The top-left corner of any shape is always exactly (0, 0), and the bottom-right is (1, 1). This guarantees that if you move, scale, or stretch your shape, the gradient stretches seamlessly with it—no complex trigonometry required!

The colorsArray Parameter: All gradients in Kilopixel share a common parameter called colorsArray to define their colors. You can provide this array in two ways:

  • Colors Only: Pass an array of just colors (e.g., ['red', 'yellow', 'blue']). Kilopixel will automatically space them out evenly from 0.0 to 1.0.
  • Color Offset Pairs: Pass an array alternating between numeric offsets (0 to 1) and colors for precise control (e.g., [0, 'red', 0.8, 'blue']).
