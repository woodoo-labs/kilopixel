8. Cross-Space Projection toLocal()

The true power of toLocal() is cross-space affine projection. It allows you to perfectly track an element nested deep inside rotating or scaled layers, without writing any trigonometry.

  • Layer Independence: You can place shapes inside completely different, independently transforming layers.
  • The Link: By using toLocal(ref.target, 'tx'), Kilopixel calculates the target's total global position (including offsets) and automatically translates it into your current coordinate space.
  • The Magic: Kilopixel's spatial reactivity engine automatically tracks matrix changes. If a layer rotates or moves, it instantly alerts any element tracking its children to update!
<pxl-stage ratio="5 / 3">
  <!-- A line on the root stage perfectly tracking two shapes -->
  <pxl-layer>
    <pxl-line 
      x1="toLocal(ref.rect1, 'tx')" y1="toLocal(ref.rect1, 'ty')" 
      x2="toLocal(ref.rect2, 'tx')" y2="toLocal(ref.rect2, 'ty')" 
      stroke="#ff00ff">
    </pxl-line>
  </pxl-layer>

  <!-- Layer 1 (Independent) -->
  <pxl-layer id="layer1" x="350" y="250" rotate="0">
    <pxl-rect id="rect1" 
      x="-150" y="-150" 
      w="200" h="200" 
      dx="100" dy="100" rotate="0"
      stroke="#f97316"></pxl-rect>
  </pxl-layer>

  <!-- Layer containing a nested Group -->
  <pxl-layer>
    <pxl-group id="group2" x="650" y="350" dx="0" dy="0" rotate="0">
      <pxl-rect id="rect2" 
        x="150" y="150" 
        w="200" h="200" 
        dx="100" dy="100" rotate="0"
        stroke="#3b82f6"></pxl-rect>
    </pxl-group>
  </pxl-layer>
</pxl-stage>
